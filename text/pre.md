## Pre-Course Employability Profile

By undertaking this course, I aim to improve my employability and secure a job by the time I graduate this year. My primary objective is to gain the qualities and experiences desired from graduate hires which are needed by employers. I acknowledge that the gaps in my current qualifications and expect to approach opportunities which help target and overcome these weaknesses for my job search.

A notable area I am lacking in is general experience looking for, applying to, and being accustomed to the job application process. Throughout the previous two years of study I have undertaken, I made a few attempts to apply to internship opportunities as well as graduate opportunities, though I had discouraged myself from continuing after failing to proceed past their initial stages. By following along with the course material and workshops, I hope to more closely identify the reasons for my lack of success so far and accordingly improve my competency to successfully meet the expectations established by employers. Attaining proper competency in these areas will sustain my confidence to ultimately enter the tech industry within a graduate role or otherwise. 

Before any major introspection, I have identified a few areas where I could improve myself; these areas are particularly related to my hard skills pertaining to having the experience and knowledge desired by employers. Within tech, a project portfolio is desirable, and I plan to further fill out my portfolio with personal projects as a means of filling out my resume, where work experience is lacking and cannot be cited. These personal projects may not entirely keep me well-rounded in being proficient in particular code work, depending on the contents of the projects themselves, so I also plan to build up a general proficiency across programming languages with problem-solving practice services such as LeetCode.

Aside from dedicating myself to practical skill development, I also want to look into networking opportunities within the course and any events which go on during the semester. Connecting with my peers as well as those in the industry will help open up my employment opportunities in the future, especially in accessing opportunities which might not necessarily be openly advertised or for collaborating on personal projects together in the future.

Overall, the ways I can personally improve my readiness for employment are mostly limited to the hard skills and technical abilities relevant to my years of study at university and the general hope that my competencies in these will allow me to find work. The primary roadblock for this, however, is the ability to successfully demonstrate these skills to employers, which I seek to remedy throughout the duration of this course. It is also relevant to note that I am looking for work opportunities rather than continuing my studies as I believe that at the point I am at now, it would be best to begin my professional career as soon as possible.
