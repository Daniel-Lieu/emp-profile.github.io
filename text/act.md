## Activity Profile

As a student studying a Bachelor of Computer Science, I made a general focus on seeking out employers who are looking for software and tech-adjacent workers, in addition to general career-seeking workshops. Due to personal reasons, I failed to attend the desired number of events.

### 1. Resume & Cover Letter Writing Online Workshop

### 13th March 2pm-3pm

This online workshop involved an overview of the general resume and cover letter writing process, providing a comprehensive look into important details of resume and cover letter writing with the goal of succeeding in passing initial job applications. The workshop described the incorporation of the STAR model to help substantiate the contents of a resume and cover letter with evidence and proof of experience. 

It is notable that this, along with much of the content present in the workshop, was similar to content present in learned course material. As such, I attained limited benefit by attending the workshop aside from refreshing and revising learned ideas.

### 2. BAE Systems Online Seminar

### 16th March 12pm-1pm

An online seminar hosted by BAE Systems helped describe the responsibilities of the company and of prospective employees. It detailed BAE Systems’ role in the defence industry and work with designing and manufacturing in collaboration with the Australian Defence Force in various domains of defence, highlighting Adelaide’s importance as a hub for Australia’s Defence Industry. 

This was followed up with a description of its internship program. This program does not particularly apply to me given that I am in my final year of study. BAE Systems’ Graduate Program was then covered, detailing the 2-year contract streams for graduate hires. The details provided by this added insight to the systems of working with mentors and graduate hire peers, as well as the development and networking opportunities afforded. 

Personal accounts of multiple graduate hires were present towards the end of the seminar. These included an overview of the experience moving interstate and working on a diverse set of roles within a project, continually learning and improving their throughput during their time at BAE Systems. While these experiences are not necessarily universal, anecdotal insight towards the experience of a new hire helps inform my future job-seeking experiences.

### 3. Casual Work Online Workshop

### 16th March 1pm-2pm

This was a workshop which went over various topics related to job searching and furthermore, casual work to undertake while job searching. Regarding this, the workshop discussed the development of skills which are desirable to employers and maximising the possible jobs a possible hire could apply themselves to. The effectiveness of transferable skills was highlighted and the importance of skills which can be developed within casual work was noted as a way to strengthen my employability within more desired roles; skills like responsiveness and communication within work, such as customer service. The use of having tangible experiences to lean on during interviews was also noted. As a result, I now take further notice of casual or part-time work which aligns well with my future job prospects, as a means of entering my target industry. 

Aside from skills, I was able to further identify the importance of qualities like location, scheduling and business size while seeking employment to be more selective of the job market to open up opportunities which suit my requirements.
